Tombolo

Your Android phone,
right on your Mac.

  • Mirror and control your phone
  • Open phone apps as Mac windows
  • Notifications, messages & calls
  • Shared clipboard and file drop
Free and open source. No account, no cloud — your devices talk to each other directly, end-to-end encrypted.

Connect your phone

Choose how Tombolo reaches your phone. You can add another connection type later in Settings.

Wi-Fi Recommended
Pair once by scanning a QR code with the Tombolo app on your phone. Reconnects automatically whenever both are on the same network.
USB cable Lowest latency
Plug in your phone and allow USB debugging. Works without Wi-Fi and unlocks Enhanced mode features.
Don’t have the phone app yet?
Scan to download Tombolo for Android — Android 10 or later.
